## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Items not glowing?**
- Make sure the pack is enabled in World Settings
- Restart your world
- All players need the pack enabled

**Not hearing sounds?**
- Check your volume in Minecraft settings
- Verify Master Volume is not muted
